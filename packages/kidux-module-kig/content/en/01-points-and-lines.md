# Points and lines

Kig is a sheet of paper where everything can move. You put points on it,
join them with lines, and then drag the points: the lines follow. The
tools are the small pictures in the bar on the left; hold the mouse over
one and Kig tells you its name.

![Two points and the line through them](images/kig-point-line.svg)

1. ![Point](images/tools/point.svg) Press **Point**, the red dot at the
   top of the left bar, then click on the canvas. A point appears. Kig
   lets go of the tool after each point: press **Point** again and click
   somewhere else for a second point.
2. ![Line by two points](images/tools/line.svg) Press **Line by two
   points** and click the first point, then the second. A line runs
   through both.
3. Now drag one of the points: press on it, move the mouse, let go. You
   need no tool for that. If Kig is still waiting for a click, press
   **Escape** first.

> [point] The line turns to follow the point. It always goes through
> both points: that is its rule.

## Names

Kig does not name points. To call a point A, right-click it, choose
**Set name**, write A and press Enter. The lessons call points by
letters so that you know which is which.

## A triangle

1. ![Segment](images/tools/segment.svg) Press **Segment** and click on
   two empty places. A segment appears: a piece of line with a point at
   each end. Kig makes the points for you.
2. Press **Segment** again and click one end of the segment, then an
   empty place: a second segment.
3. Join the two loose ends with a third segment. You have a triangle.
4. Drag a corner. The triangle changes shape but stays a triangle.

::: try
Make a square with four segments. Then drag one corner. Is it still a
square? In the lesson about parallels you will learn how to make one
that stays square.
:::
