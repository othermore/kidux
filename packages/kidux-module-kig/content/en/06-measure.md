# Measure it

Kig can tell you how long a segment is and how big an angle is, and the
numbers change as you drag.

![A triangle with its three angles marked](images/kig-angles.svg)

1. ![Segment](images/tools/segment.svg) Draw a triangle with three
   segments, as in the first lesson.
2. ![Angle by three points](images/tools/angle.svg) Press **Angle by
   three points** and click three corners in turn: the middle click is
   the corner the angle is at. A small arc marks it.
3. Right-click the arc, choose **Add text label**, then **Angle in
   degrees**. The number appears beside the arc.
4. Do the same at the other two corners.
5. Drag a corner and watch the three numbers.

> [cheer] Add the three numbers. However you drag, they always make 180.
> Every triangle in the world does this.

## Lengths

1. Right-click a segment, **Add text label**, then **Length**.
2. Drag an end and watch the number.

::: try
Press **Perpendicular** to make a right angle, and draw a triangle on
it. Measure the three sides. Multiply each by itself: the two short ones
added give the long one. That is Pythagoras, and Kig lets you check it.
:::
