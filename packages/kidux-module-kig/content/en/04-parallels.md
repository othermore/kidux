# Perpendicular and parallel

Two lines are *perpendicular* when they cross like a plus sign. Two
lines are *parallel* when they never cross, like the rails of a train.
Kig draws both for you, and they keep their rule when you move things.

![A line, a point, and the perpendicular and parallel through it](images/kig-parallels.svg)

@mission missions/parallels.kig

1. Open the mission. There is a line and a point P off it.
2. ![Perpendicular](images/tools/perpendicular.svg) Press
   **Perpendicular**, click the line, then click P. A line through P
   crosses the first at a right angle.
3. ![Parallel](images/tools/parallel.svg) Press **Parallel**, click the
   line, then click P. A line through P that never meets the first.
4. Drag P. Both new lines move with it and keep their rule.
5. Now drag one of the two points the first line goes through. The
   first line turns, and the other two turn with it.

> [think] The parallel and the perpendicular are *made from* the first
> line. Change the first, and they change. That is what Kig is for.

## A square that stays square

1. ![Segment](images/tools/segment.svg) Press **Segment** and click on
   two empty places. Call its ends A and B: right-click each, **Set
   name**.
2. ![Perpendicular](images/tools/perpendicular.svg) Press
   **Perpendicular**, click the segment, then A. Again: **Perpendicular**,
   the segment, then B.
3. ![Circle by centre and point](images/tools/circlebcp.svg) Press
   **Circle by centre and point**, click A, then B.
4. ![Intersect](images/tools/curvelineintersection.svg) Press
   **Intersect**, click the circle, then the perpendicular through A.
   Kig marks where they cross, above and below: call the upper one D.
5. ![Parallel](images/tools/parallel.svg) Press **Parallel**, click the
   segment, then D.
6. ![Intersect](images/tools/curvelineintersection.svg) Press
   **Intersect**, click that parallel, then the perpendicular through B.
   Call the new point C.
7. ![Segment](images/tools/segment.svg) Join A, B, C and D with
   segments, all the way round. Drag A or B: it stays a square.

> [point] When several things are under the mouse, Kig asks *Which
> object?* Pick the one you mean.

::: try
Hide the lines and the circle you used: right-click each, **Hide**. Keep
only the square. It still works.
:::
