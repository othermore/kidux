# The words of photography

The main words of the guide that a photographer uses, in the order of
the alphabet, with the chapter it is taught in.

- **Aperture**: the hole in the lens that the blades make bigger or
  smaller, written as an f-number. Chapter 7.
- **Autofocus**: the camera focusing by itself, on what is under its
  square. Chapters 16 and 17.
- **Back light**: light that comes from behind what you photograph.
  Chapter 25.
- **Bokeh**: the soft discs that points of light become, out of focus.
  Chapter 18.
- **Bulb**: a time as long as you hold the button down. Chapter 13.
- **Camera obscura**: a dark room or box with a hole, where the world
  appears upside down. Chapter 1.
- **Composition**: where you put things in the picture. Chapters 21 to 23.
- **Depth of field**: how deep the slice is that looks sharp, in front of
  and behind what you focused on. Chapter 18.
- **Exposure**: how much light reaches the sensor for one picture.
  Chapter 5.
- **Exposure compensation**: the +/−, telling the camera to make a
  picture brighter or darker than it measured. Chapter 5.
- **F-number**: the aperture written as a fraction: F8 is a small hole,
  F1.8 a big one. Chapter 7.
- **Fill-in flash**: a little flash to light a face in shadow, in the
  daytime. Chapter 15.
- **Focal length**: how long the lens is, in millimetres: small for wide
  angle, big for telephoto. Chapter 20.
- **Focus**: choosing which distance is sharp. Chapters 2 and 16.
- **Golden hour**: the hour after sunrise and before sunset, with low,
  warm, soft light. Chapter 25.
- **Histogram**: a mountain showing how many dark and how many bright parts a
  picture has. Chapter 5.
- **ISO**: how much the camera turns up the light it caught. Chapter 8.
- **Lens**: the glass that bends light to make a sharp picture.
  Chapter 2.
- **Macro**: photographs from very close. Chapter 19.
- **Metering**: how the camera measures the light. Chapter 10.
- **Noise**: the grain in a picture taken at a high ISO. Chapter 8.
- **Overexposed**: too bright, too much light. Chapter 5.
- **Pixel**: one of the sensor's little squares, one dot of the picture.
  Chapter 3.
- **Playback**: looking at the pictures on the camera. Chapter 28.
- **Rule of thirds**: putting things on the lines that cut the picture in
  three. Chapter 21.
- **Sensor**: the back wall of the camera, which catches the light.
  Chapter 3.
- **Shutter**: the little door that opens to let the light in for a
  time. Chapter 6.
- **Shutter speed**: how long the shutter stays open, the time.
  Chapter 6.
- **Silhouette**: a black shape against a bright background. Chapter 25.
- **Stop**: twice as much light, or half as much. Chapter 5.
- **Telephoto**, or tele: the zoom at its longest, seeing a small piece of the
  world. Chapter 20.
- **Tripod**: a stand with three legs that holds the camera still.
  Chapter 4.
- **Underexposed**: too dark, too little light. Chapter 5.
- **White balance**: telling the camera which white is white, so that the
  colours are right in any light. Chapter 26.
- **Wide angle**: the zoom at its shortest, taking in a wide view.
  Chapter 20.
- **Zoom**: changing how much of the world the lens sees. Chapter 20.
