# REM: notes to yourself

A program you wrote last week can be hard to understand today. `REM`
lines are notes for people: the computer skips them.

```basic keys=4,Enter
10 REM THE FISH SHOP
20 REM BY ME
30 LET PRICE = 3
40 INPUT "HOW MANY FISH? "; N
50 REM WORK OUT THE COST
60 PRINT "THAT IS "; N * PRICE; " COINS"
```

An apostrophe, `'`, is a shorter `REM`, and it can go at the end of a
line too:

```basic
10 LET PRICE = 3 ' THE PRICE OF ONE FISH
20 ' A WHOLE LINE OF NOTE
30 PRINT PRICE
```

> [point] Good notes say *why*, not just *what*. Future you will be
> grateful.

## Keep trying

::: adult
Notes are a habit worth building early. A program read aloud, notes and
all, is also a good way for a child to explain what they made.
:::
