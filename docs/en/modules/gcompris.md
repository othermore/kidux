# GCompris

*[Versión en español](../../es/modules/gcompris.md)* · [Back to the user guide](../user-guide.md#9-learning-modules)

**GCompris** is over a hundred small games for the youngest children: reading and writing, counting, colours and shapes, and learning to use the keyboard and the mouse.

| | |
|---|---|
| **For** | Children from 2 to 10 |
| **Needs** | The internet the first few times, to fetch its voices and some pictures. Nothing afterwards. |
| **Program** | [GCompris](https://gcompris.net/), by the GCompris community, under the GPL-3 licence, from Debian |

## What it is

GCompris is a collection of activities, each one a little game, and each one teaching one thing. A child learns the letters by hearing them, counts with pictures, sorts colours and shapes, and gets used to the keyboard and the mouse. It speaks the child's language, with voices that read the words out loud.

![GCompris](../../images/en/module-gcompris.png)

## What is inside

More than a hundred activities, sorted by what they teach:

- reading and writing: the letters, their sounds, words and the alphabet;
- numbers: counting, adding, taking away, the clock and money;
- colours and shapes, memory games and puzzles;
- the computer itself: the keyboard, the mouse, drawing and simple logic.

Each activity says, with a picture, which ages it is for. GCompris takes the whole screen above Kidux's bar, and the bar's **Home** takes the child back to their other things.

## Good to know

- Its voices and some of its pictures are not on the computer at first. GCompris fetches them by itself from the internet the first times it is used, so it needs a connection then. It works without them until they have arrived. The panel says so before GCompris is installed.
- What a child draws or writes in an activity stays inside GCompris. Nothing is saved in the child's folders.
