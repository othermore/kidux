# GCompris

*[Versión en español](../../es/modules/gcompris.md)* · [Back to the user guide](../user-guide.md#9-learning-modules)

**GCompris** is a comprehensive educational software suite featuring more than a hundred interactive learning activities for children aged 2 to 10, covering literacy, mathematics, science, geography, and foundational mouse and keyboard skills.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Recommended age** | Children from 2 to 10 years old |
| **Internet connection** | Only required during initial runs to download audio voice packs and media assets. Once cached, the suite operates completely offline. |
| **Software used** | [GCompris](https://gcompris.net/), developed by the international GCompris open source community under the GPL-3 licence, packaged from Debian |

## What it is and how it works

GCompris brings together an extensive collection of educational mini-games tailored to child development stages. Young learners can hear spoken letter sounds, match counts with illustrated items, sort shapes and colours, and develop fine motor skills with the mouse and keyboard at their own comfortable pace.

The application is fully translated and accompanied by spoken voice recordings in the child's native language, enabling autonomous play and exploration even before the child has mastered reading.

## Included activity categories

Activities are grouped into distinct learning areas, easily identified by playful icons:

- **Reading and writing**: alphabet exploration, phonics, word creation, and vocabulary puzzles.
- **Mathematics and logic**: counting, addition, subtraction, analog and digital clock reading, and currency recognition.
- **Science and geography**: world maps, water cycle experiments, and introductory physics phenomena.
- **Computer discovery**: guided exercises for moving the cursor, clicking, double-clicking, typing, and creative drawing.

Each activity displays small difficulty stars and age indicators. GCompris runs in full screen above the bottom taskbar; pressing the bar's **Home** button returns smoothly to the child's main launcher.

## Important notes

- **Voice pack downloads**: To conserve initial disk space, narrator voice recordings and select media assets download automatically in the background when an activity requiring them is first opened. The adult control panel displays a reminder before installation.
- **Privacy and data saving**: Drawings, custom configurations, and completed activity stars are stored internally within GCompris without leaving extraneous files in the child's personal home folders.
