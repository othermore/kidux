# Scratch

*[Versión en español](../../es/modules/scratch.md)* · [Back to the user guide](../user-guide.md#9-learning-modules)

**Scratch** is the editor children use at school to make games, stories and animations by snapping blocks together.

| | |
|---|---|
| **For** | Children from 8 to 16 |
| **Before it** | ScratchJr |
| **Needs** | The internet to open its library of characters, backdrops and sounds. Everything else works without it. |
| **Program** | [Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/), by the Scratch Foundation, under the AGPL-3 licence, built by Kidux to run on the computer |

## What it is

It is Scratch's own editor, in the child's language, running on the computer itself. There is no Scratch website community around it: nothing a child makes is shared, and nobody else can see it.

![Scratch](../../images/en/module-scratch.png)

## What is inside

- the editor, with the stage, the sprites and every block Scratch has;
- the library of characters, backdrops and sounds, fetched from the Scratch Foundation's servers when a child opens it;
- the paint and sound editors;
- the tutorials' texts. Their videos do not play, because they are on a website, and a module shows only its own pages.

## The child's files

To keep a project, the child chooses **File**, then **Save to your computer**. A window of the child's folders opens, in **Downloads**, to choose where to keep the project and under what name, and **Save** keeps it. **File**, then **Load from your computer**, opens the same window to pick a project from.

A project saved from Scratch opens in TurboWarp too, and the other way round.
